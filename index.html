Philosophy

Fitness that holds up under real life, not just in the gym.

Most programmes are built for the mirror. This one is built for the decades ahead of it: knees that still bend well at sixty, energy that lasts past 3pm, and a body that recovers from a hard week instead of accumulating one.

Training is only one part of that picture. Sleep, stress, nutrition and movement quality are coached with the same rigour as the barbell work.

  • Functional

    Movement patterns that carry over to how you actually live and move. No isolated machine work for its own sake.

  • Evidence-based

    Programming grounded in current research rather than trends: periodisation, progressive overload and recovery science, applied properly.

  • Holistic

    Sleep, nutrition and stress are coached alongside training, because none of them work in isolation.

  • Long game

    Built around what you can sustain for years, not what looks impressive for six weeks.

Method

How the coaching actually works.

Four stages. The fourth feeds straight back into the first.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    A proper starting point. Training history, movement screening, sleep and stress patterns, and an honest read of where your body is right now.

  2. 02

    Programme design

    A structured block built around your goals, your schedule and any physical considerations. Never a generic template.

  3. 03

    Weekly coaching

    Check-ins, adjustments and accountability every week. The programme evolves as your data and your life do.

  4. 04

    Review & progress

    Regular reassessment against real markers: strength, body composition, energy, and how you feel day to day.

Who this is for

For people playing a longer game.

  • You train, or want to, and the last few programmes didn't survive a busy month.
  • You'd rather be strong and mobile at sixty than lean for one summer.
  • You want one coach looking at sleep, food and stress alongside the sessions.
  • You want to be told what to change this week, not handed a PDF.
