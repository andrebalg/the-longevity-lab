Philosophy

Fitness that holds up in real life.

This programme is built for the decades ahead. Knees that still bend well at sixty. Energy that lasts past 3pm. A body that recovers from a hard week.

Training is one part of the picture. Sleep, stress, nutrition and movement quality are coached with the same rigour as the barbell work.

  • Functional

    Movement patterns that carry over to how you live and move. Every exercise has to earn its place in the programme.

  • Evidence-based

    Programming grounded in current research. Periodisation, progressive overload and recovery science, applied properly.

  • Holistic

    Sleep, nutrition and stress are coached alongside training. Each one affects the others.

  • Long game

    Built around what you can sustain for years. The results compound.

Expertise

What the coaching covers.

Four areas, coached together. Each one supports the other three.

  • Lean muscle

    Building lean muscle with progressive resistance training and the protein to support it. Training volume is set to what your body can recover from, so the muscle you build stays.

  • Strength

    Strength work on the fundamental patterns: squat, hinge, push, pull and carry. Technique comes first. Load is added week by week.

  • Recovery

    Recovery is programmed with the same care as training. Sleep, deload weeks and daily movement are written into the plan.

  • Nutrition

    Nutrition that fits your life and feeds the training. Precision Nutrition methods, applied to real meals and real schedules.

Method

How the coaching actually works.

Four stages. The fourth feeds straight back into the first.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    A proper starting point. Training history, movement screening, sleep and stress patterns, and an honest read of where your body is right now.

  2. 02

    Programme design

    A structured block built around your goals, your schedule and any physical considerations, written from scratch for you.

  3. 03

    Weekly coaching

    Check-ins, adjustments and accountability every week. The programme evolves as your data and your life do.

  4. 04

    Review & progress

    Regular reassessment against real markers: strength, body composition, energy, and how you feel day to day.

Who this is for

For people playing a longer game.

  • You train, or want to, and you need a programme that survives a busy month.
  • You want to be strong and mobile at sixty.
  • You want one coach looking at sleep, food and stress alongside the sessions.
  • You want clear instructions on what to change this week.
